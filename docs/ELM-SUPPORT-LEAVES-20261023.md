# Elm LAB review: branch support and leaf twigs, 2026-10-23

The owner confirmed that both elms' leaves and shadows stopped shimmering. This
iteration preserves the accepted foliage materials, review-stand temporal AA,
shadow settings, positions and crown silhouettes.

The first elm now carries oversized child branches through thicker upstream
supports before the major wood surfaces are fused. All 422 child starts have an
effective parent radius at least 1.15 times the child radius. Its accepted bark
images remain unchanged; world triplanar scale changes from 0.42 to 0.17 so the
pattern appears larger and repeats less frequently.

Both models receive an additional wood mesh of fine three-dimensional twigs.
Each retained leaf card is rooted through a shared twig network into the original
wood. Transparent gaps between disconnected silhouettes within a card also get
fine bridges. Nearby bare shoot tips continue into existing leaf clusters. No
additional leaves are removed, moved, resized or retextured. The earlier Elm2
upper-crown pruning remains the reference input.
