# Single elm visual trial in Ardee

The owner supplied `Olmo_DAoC_Enhanced (2).zip` on 21 October. Its GLB has 30,162 triangles in two meshes/materials (bark and alpha-cut foliage); it is a user-supplied prototype, not the previously rejected Meshy candidate. Supplied Python is retained as source data and is not executed. No external download or generation is required.

`enhanced/trees/prepare_elm.gd` imports the GLB through Godot, adds a Z-up to Y-up wrapper, uniformly matches the stock elm's above-origin height and saves a private PackedScene with mipmapped textures. The received GLB and PNGs are unchanged. The scene, source hash, stock/zone hashes, bounds and uniform scale are recorded under ignored `.enhanced-session/elm_trial/bundles/user_20261021`.

`elm_trial.gd` replaces only fixture **207:1109**, model `models/helm.gltf`, at Godot-local **1395.2 / 130.0875 / 1302.4** (DAoC local X/Y **55808/52096**, near the Ardee well). The fixture yaw (-66 degrees), placement and scale (1.32) remain intact. Exactly one matching original MultiMesh slot is required; it is hidden in memory and restored byte-for-byte on A/B or cleanup. The new two-part tree uses static MultiMesh rendering with the original object's draw distance. All original collision shapes, source models, textures, fixture tables and databases remain unchanged. It does not replace Elm2 or any other tree.

The LAB entry point attaches the optional controller only after the scene has loaded. An absent, disabled, changed or invalid manifest retains the stock tree. The overlay follows RegionWorld unload/reload and preserves the original/new choice. **Ctrl+F9** toggles the one tree in the LAB; the standalone `preview_elm.gd` has A/B buttons, Space to toggle and Tab for three camera positions. Private activation is reversible without editing stock assets.

Validation uses the actual renderer: same-camera/light A/B captures from three views, one original slot/one new tree, unchanged physics shape signatures, exact restoration of every stock fixture batch transform, region attachment/unload/reload, and rejection of an invalid scene hash. The Dummy headless renderer did not expose a matching stock tree slot, so the integration checks were performed with the normal Vulkan renderer. The captures are a standalone zone without server/NPCs, not a complete gameplay/session test. At 1440x900 the sampled original and new views stayed near the 60 FPS cap in this run; that does not establish the cost of replacing a whole forest, especially with another client running.

The prototype is about 113 times the stock model's 266 triangles and has no dedicated distance LODs. Its trunk is dark and its branching is coarse; these are visible properties of the supplied prototype, not reasons to silently alter its art. Scale, species, distribution, vegetation streaming and all other scenery are outside this trial. No claim is made that this is a finished tree suitable for global deployment.
